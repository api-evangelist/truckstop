A TMS for freight brokers is software that manages the full load lifecycle in one place, from creating and dispatching a load to tracking it, generating paperwork, and invoicing. A transportation management system keeps carrier and shipper records, rates, and documents together, so your team works from one source instead of spreadsheets, email, and memory.
